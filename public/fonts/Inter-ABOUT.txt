Inter — provenance and licence
==============================

`inter-latin-variable.woff2` is:

    Inter, version 4.001 (git 66647c0bb)
    Rasmus Andersson — https://rsms.me/inter/
    SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 — https://openfontlicense.org

It is the **latin subset** of the variable font, as served by Google Fonts, taken
unmodified from:

    https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@400;700

One file covers every weight from 100 to 900 — Google serves the same variable
font for each weight you ask for, so requesting 400 and 700 fetches one 48 KB
file, not two. That is why the @font-face in revival.css declares
`font-weight: 100 900` rather than a single value.

Unmodified, so it keeps its name. Vendored here rather than loaded from Google's
CDN, so the site keeps having no third-party requests — which is the same reason
Arvo and the wordmark are vendored.
